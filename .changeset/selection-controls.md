Selection controls: Checkbox (with an indeterminate state), RadioGroup and RadioGroupItem, a themed Switch whose thumb slides with the motion Tokens and jumps under Reduce Motion, and Chip with ChipGroup (single or multiple), all with a haptic tick. Adds the `checkbox.pressed`, `radio.pressed`, `switch.pressed` and `chip.pressed` Style Slots and the `checkbox-demo`, `radio-group-demo`, `switch-demo` and `chip-demo` examples.
